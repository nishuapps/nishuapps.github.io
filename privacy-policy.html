1. Overview & Key Points

GoNotes Pro is a personal note-taking app developed by NishuApps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

✅
The short version: Your notes stay on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. The only optional network activity is backing up your own data to your own Google Drive — and that backup is encrypted before it leaves your device.
Question Answer
Do you collect personal data? No — no analytics, no accounts required
Do you share data with third parties? No — data never leaves your device unless you opt into Drive backup
Do you show ads? No — the app is completely ad-free
Is there analytics/crash tracking? None — no Firebase, Crashlytics, or any analytics SDK
Is Google Sign-In required? Optional — only for Google Drive backup/restore
Are notes encrypted? Yes — AES-256-GCM encryption with hardware-backed keys
Is the app suitable for children? 13+ — general audience; not specifically designed for children under 13

4. How Data Is Stored & Protected

Local Storage

All notes, images, and settings are stored in the app's private local storage on your device. Other apps cannot access this data. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.

🔑
Cloud backup is disabled by default: android:allowBackup="false" and android:fullBackupContent="false" are set, so Android's automatic cloud backup system never copies your notes without your explicit action.

Encryption (AES-256-GCM)

GoNotes Pro uses AES-256-GCM encryption for protected note data and for encrypted Google Drive backups.

  • Local note data: encrypted using an Android Keystore-managed encryption key. On supported devices, the Keystore can be backed by secure hardware such as a Trusted Execution Environment (TEE).
  • Google Drive backups: use a separate randomly generated encryption key. That backup key is protected using a key derived from the signed-in Google account ID with PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256.
  • Encryption keys and plaintext note content are not intentionally transmitted to NishuApps-operated servers.

Image Storage

Photos you attach to notes are saved in the app's private internal directory. Camera images are automatically converted to efficient WebP format and the original heavy file is deleted. Image file paths stored inside note JSON are also individually encrypted.

Reminder Data

Reminder times and associated note IDs are stored in a separate SharedPreferences file ("MyNotesAlarms") on your device. This data is never transmitted externally.

Screen Security

When you enter a PIN to unlock a note, the app enables the FLAG_SECURE window flag, which prevents screenshots and screen recording of that screen.

5. Google Sign-In & Google Drive Sync

⚠️
This feature is entirely optional. GoNotes Pro works fully offline without a Google account. Drive sync is only available if you choose to sign in.

What Happens When You Sign In

  1. You are redirected to Google's standard OAuth sign-in screen (handled by Google, not by us).
  2. GoNotes Pro requests only the DRIVE_APPDATA scope — a restricted, app-specific folder in your Google Drive that only GoNotes Pro can read. No other app (including other Google apps) can see this folder.
  3. After you sign in and enable Google Drive synchronization, GoNotes Pro may automatically back up your notes and associated images to this private, app-specific Drive area.

Google Drive Scope Used

OAuth Scope What It Allows What We Access
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata Read/write access to a private, app-specific folder only — not your full Drive Only the backup file created by GoNotes Pro itself

We do not request access to your full Drive, your files, your emails, your contacts, your photos library, or any other Google service.

Backup Encryption

Before any data is uploaded to Google Drive, it is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your Google account ID. The encrypted file is meaningless without your account. The same account on any device can decrypt the backup.

Revoking Access

You can revoke GoNotes Pro's access to your Google Drive at any time:

  1. Open GoNotes Pro → Side Menu → Sign Out
  2. Or: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove GoNotes Pro

Revoking access deletes the Drive connection from the app and removes the stored account email. Your local notes are not affected.

Google's Privacy Policy

When you use Google Sign-In, Google's own privacy practices also apply: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other unrelated third parties.

When you use Google services with GoNotes Pro:

  • Google Sign-In: Google receives the account and authentication information required to sign you in. This is handled through Google's own authentication service.
  • Google Drive backup and synchronization (optional, user-enabled): encrypted backup data may be uploaded to your own private Google Drive appdata folder. This data is inaccessible to us or unrelated third parties through GoNotes Pro's Drive access.

Sharing via Your Own Actions

When you use the "Share Note", "Share as PDF", or "Share as Text File" features, you choose the destination app (WhatsApp, email, etc.) yourself. GoNotes Pro simply hands the file to Android's share system — we do not send data to any server.

Legal Disclosure

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority. As GoNotes Pro stores all data locally on your device, we do not hold your notes on any server and therefore have no notes to disclose.

10. Security Measures

GoNotes Pro implements multiple layers of security to protect your data:

Security Feature Details
AES-256-GCM Encryption All notes are encrypted at rest using AES-256 in GCM mode. Authentication tags are verified on decryption to detect tampered data.
Hardware-Backed Keys (AndroidKeyStore) Encryption keys are stored in the Android Keystore system, backed by a hardware Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) on supported devices. Keys cannot be extracted.
PBKDF2 Key Derivation The backup encryption key is derived using PBKDF2WithHmacSHA256, making brute-force attacks computationally expensive.
Biometric Authentication Biometric (fingerprint/face) is available to unlock the Note Vault. Biometric data never leaves the device's Secure Enclave.
Root Detection The app detects rooted devices and disables biometric security features on them, since root access can compromise biometric authentication.
HTTPS Only Cleartext HTTP is explicitly blocked (android:usesCleartextTraffic="false"). All network communication uses TLS/HTTPS.
No Auto-Backup android:allowBackup="false" prevents Android's built-in cloud backup from copying notes without your knowledge.
Screenshot Protection The PIN entry screen sets FLAG_SECURE, preventing screenshots, screen recording, and preview in the app switcher.
Image Path Encryption File paths of attached images stored inside note JSON are also encrypted individually.
Drive App-Folder Scope Only Google Drive access is restricted to the private appdata scope — the narrowest possible Drive permission.

While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong device PIN/passcode for an additional layer of protection.
