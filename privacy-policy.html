Privacy Policy

GoNotes Pro — developed by NishuApps

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the GoNotes Pro Android application ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the App. The App is developed and maintained by NishuApps.

GoNotes Pro is a notes application. Its core features — creating, editing, storing, searching, and organizing notes — work entirely on your device. An optional Google account sign-in is available only for users who choose to back up and restore their notes using Google Drive.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to read it carefully.

Information We Handle

The App handles the following categories of information, all of which are provided by you or created through your use of the App:

  • Notes and note content — the titles, text, and content you create inside the App, including notes organized into folders and categories.
  • Images — images you attach to your notes using the camera or your device's photo gallery.
  • Reminder information — reminder and alarm times you set for individual notes, and the note titles associated with those reminders.
  • Preferences and settings — app preferences such as your chosen theme, view mode, sort order, and categories.
  • Google account information (if you choose to sign in) — your Google account email address, display name, and profile photo, as described in the next section.

The App does not collect device location, contacts, calendar data, SMS or call information, financial information, health information, or advertising identifiers. The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Google Account / Authentication

GoNotes Pro does not create or maintain a separate GoNotes Pro user account. There is no registration system, no email-and-password sign-up, and no GoNotes-specific account profile.

Instead, the App optionally uses your existing Google Account through Google Sign-In for two purposes only:

  • Authenticating you with Google, and
  • Accessing a private, app-specific area of Google Drive to back up and restore your notes (described below).

When you sign in with Google, the App receives basic profile information from Google, including your email address, display name, and profile photo. Your email address is stored on your device in encrypted form so the App can identify which account was last used. Your display name and profile photo are used only to personalize the sign-in screen within the App.

The App does not own, control, or manage your Google Account. It cannot delete your Google Account, and it does not retain your Google password. Authentication tokens are handled by Google's own services on your device.

Sign-in is completely optional. If you do not sign in, all App features other than cloud backup and restore continue to work normally.

Google Drive Backup & Synchronization

Google Drive backup and synchronization are available when you sign in with your Google Account. After your first sign-in, GoNotes Pro automatically begins backing up your existing notes and associated images to the app-specific area of your Google Drive. While you remain signed in, newly created notes, associated images, and changes to existing notes may be automatically synchronized to your Google Drive backup.

When you sign in again after previously using the backup feature, GoNotes Pro first restores available data from your existing GoNotes Pro backup and then uploads local data that is not already present in that backup.

GoNotes Pro also provides manual backup and restore options. You can use these options if automatic backup or restore does not complete or if you want to manually initiate a backup or restore operation.

If you sign out while a backup is in progress, the backup may not complete. Signing out does not delete your local notes or your existing GoNotes Pro backup from Google Drive.

GoNotes Pro uses its app-specific Google Drive storage for its backup data and does not access, modify, or delete your other personal files or folders in Google Drive.

User-Generated Content

The content you create in the App remains under your control. This includes:

  • Notes — titles and body text.
  • Images — photos you attach to notes.
  • Reminders and alarms — reminder times you attach to notes, and the titles shown with those reminders.
  • Backup data — the encrypted backup file described above, which contains your notes and images.

This content is stored locally on your device. If you choose to sign in and use the backup feature, a copy of your notes and images is uploaded to your Google Drive AppData folder in encrypted form.

Your user-generated content is not shared with any third party. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or analytics.

Local Storage

The App stores data on your device in the following ways:

  • Notes and categories are stored in the App's private storage using encryption, with keys protected by your device's secure key storage.
  • Images attached to notes are stored as image files in the App's private application folder on your device. These files are kept private to the App by the Android operating system.
  • Reminder and alarm information (reminder times and note titles) is stored locally on your device so that reminders can be shown at the scheduled time.
  • Preferences (such as theme, view mode, and sort order) are stored locally on your device.
  • Your email address (if you sign in with Google) is stored locally in encrypted form.

We use reasonable technical safeguards to help protect the information stored by the App, including encryption for note data and your email address where supported by your device. As with any software, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

How Information Is Used

The App uses the information it handles for the following purposes:

  • To provide the core note-taking functionality: creating, editing, storing, searching, organizing, and displaying your notes.
  • To attach, display, and share images you choose to add to your notes.
  • To display reminder notifications at the times you set.
  • To provide optional cloud backup and restore when you sign in with your Google Account.
  • To personalize the sign-in screen with your Google display name and profile photo.

The App does not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than those described in this Policy.

Data Sharing / Third Parties

GoNotes Pro does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

However, because the App integrates with Google services, certain information is provided to Google when you choose to use those features:

  • When you sign in with Google, Google receives authentication information in accordance with Google's own privacy practices. The App also receives the basic profile information described above.
  • When you use Google Drive backup, your encrypted notes and images are uploaded to the app-specific folder of your Google Drive account. This information is processed by Google to provide the Drive service.

These exchanges happen only as part of the Google sign-in and Drive backup functionality you choose to use, and are governed by Google's privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy.

Apart from these Google services, the App does not transmit your information to any external server or third party.

Permissions

The App requests the following permissions, each of which is used only for the purpose described:

  • Camera — used only when you choose to take a photo to attach to a note. Photos are stored in the App's private storage.
  • Notifications — used to show reminder notifications at the times you set for your notes.
  • Internet and network state — used to communicate with Google services when you choose to sign in or use cloud backup. Your notes function fully without an internet connection.
  • Boot (startup) and wake lock — used to reschedule your note reminders after a device restart and to show them on time.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, call logs, SMS, calendar, or media storage.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information:

  • Note data and your email address are stored using encryption with keys protected by your device's secure key storage where supported.
  • Cloud backups are encrypted before they are uploaded to Google Drive.
  • Communication with Google services is protected using secure HTTPS connections.
  • Optional PIN or biometric lock protection is available for individual notes.
  • The App does not back up its data to Google's device backup service, so your notes are not uploaded outside the App through automatic OS backup.

While we strive to use reasonable safeguards, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

Data Retention

The App retains information for as long as necessary to provide the functionality you request, unless you choose to delete it using the deletion features described below.

  • Local data (notes, images, preferences, and reminder information) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backup remains in your Google Drive AppData folder until you delete it using the App, using Google's tools, or as long as you keep it.

We do not enforce any fixed retention period because the App has no servers of its own — data is not stored by us, only on your device and, if you choose, in your Google Drive.

Data Deletion

You can delete your GoNotes Pro data from inside the App at any time.

Delete Local & Cloud Data (available in the App under Privacy & Data):

  • Deletes all notes and local App data from your device.
  • Deletes the images and files associated with your notes from the App's private storage.
  • Deletes the GoNotes Pro backup file from your Google Drive AppData folder.

You can also delete individual notes using the App's note deletion and recycle bin features.

Please note the distinction between these actions:

  • Deleting GoNotes Pro data removes the data the App controls — your local notes and the App's cloud backup file.
  • Deleting your Google Account is controlled by Google, not by GoNotes Pro. The App cannot delete your Google Account.
  • Deleting your personal Google Drive files is controlled by you through your Google Account. The App does not access or delete files in your Drive outside its own app-specific folder.

Logout / Disconnect

The App provides a Logout option for users who have signed in with Google.

Logout does the following:

  • Signs you out of Google authentication in the App.
  • Revokes the App's access to your Google account.
  • Stops automatic synchronization of your backup.
  • Removes your Google profile information from the sign-in screen.

Logout does not delete your notes, does not delete your images, and does not delete your existing Google Drive backup. If you want to delete that data, use the data deletion features described above.

Children's Privacy

GoNotes Pro is a general-purpose notes application and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at the address below so we can assist.

User Choices

You have the following choices regarding your information:

  • Use the App without cloud backup — the App works fully on your device without signing in to Google.
  • Manual Backup & Restore — you can manually start a backup or restore operation when needed, including when automatic synchronization does not complete.
  • Logout — disconnect your Google account from the App at any time.
  • Delete individual notes — using the App's note deletion and recycle bin features.
  • Delete all local and cloud data — using the Delete Local & Cloud Data feature described above.
  • Contact us — if you have questions about your data or this Policy, you can reach us at the email address below.
